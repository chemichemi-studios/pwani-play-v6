PWANI CONNECT — COLLABORATE • NETWORK • GROW

You are a Senior UX Designer, Social Product Architect, Community Systems Designer, and Design Systems Lead.

Design a high-fidelity, production-ready, fully clickable mobile-first prototype for Pwani Connect, the professional networking and collaboration platform within Pwani Play – Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.

Use Auto Layout, reusable components, design tokens, accessibility best practices, responsive layouts, and realistic prototype interactions.

The experience should combine the professionalism of LinkedIn, the collaboration of Slack, the communities of Discord, and the simplicity of WhatsApp Communities, while remaining focused on Africa's creative economy.

Enable creators and organizations to:

Build professional networks
Discover collaborators
Join creative communities
Share updates
Form production teams
Host discussions
Exchange knowledge
Organize events

Grow careers

The experience should encourage meaningful collaboration rather than endless scrolling.
