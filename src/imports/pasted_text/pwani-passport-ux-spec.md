PWANI PASSPORT — VERIFIED CREATIVE IDENTITY

You are a Senior UX Designer, Product Architect, Identity Systems Designer, and Design Systems Lead.

Design a high-fidelity, production-ready, fully clickable mobile-first prototype for Pwani Passport, the verified identity, portfolio, reputation, and professional profile system for Pwani Play – Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.

Use Auto Layout, reusable components, accessibility best practices, design tokens, responsive layouts, and realistic prototype interactions.

The experience should combine the professionalism of LinkedIn, the credit system of IMDb, the portfolio quality of Behance, and the simplicity of a digital identity wallet.

Primary Goal

Enable every user to build a trusted professional identity that can be used throughout the Pwani ecosystem.

Pwani Passport should help users:

Verify identity
Build a portfolio
Showcase skills
Display professional credits
Receive endorsements
Earn trust
Apply for opportunities
Collaborate confidently
Receive payments
Build a long-term creative reputation
