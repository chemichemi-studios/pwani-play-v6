PWANI PLAY — STREAMING EXPERIENCE MODULE

You are a Senior Product Designer, UX Architect, and Design Systems Lead creating the flagship streaming experience for Pwani Play — Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.

Design a high-fidelity, production-ready, fully clickable mobile prototype using Auto Layout, reusable components, variables, responsive layouts, accessibility best practices, and realistic prototype interactions.

The experience should feel comparable to Netflix, Disney+, DramaBox, YouTube, and Spotify, while reflecting African storytelling, creativity, and culture.

Core Objective

Create an engaging streaming platform where users can:

Discover content
Watch movies and series
Download episodes for offline viewing
Earn Reward Coins
Build watchlists
Continue watching across devices
Interact with creators
Share content
Receive AI-powered recommendations
Upgrade to Premium
Support creators

The experience should emphasize speed, immersion, and simplicity.

Auto Layout & Responsive Rules
Use Auto Layout on every screen.
Apply an 8-point spacing grid.
Build reusable color, typography, spacing, and radius variables.
Design for mobile portrait first while ensuring layouts adapt cleanly to larger devices.

Include transitions such as fades, slides, bottom sheets, pull-to-refresh, loading overlays, download progress animations, and success confirmations.

Expected Deliverable

Generate a premium streaming experience consisting of approximately 50–70 fully connected mobile screens, complete with reusable components, realistic states, offline-first behaviors, Reward Coin mechanics, Premium subscription flows, AI-powered recommendations, and a polished clickable prototype that reflects the quality of the world's leading streaming platforms while showcasing the unique identity of Pwani Play: Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.
