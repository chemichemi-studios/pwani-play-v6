PWANI STUDIO — CREATE • MANAGE • PUBLISH • MONETIZE

You are a Senior Product Designer, Product Architect, UX Researcher, and Design Systems Lead.

Design a high-fidelity, production-ready, fully clickable mobile-first prototype for Pwani Studio, the creator operating system within Pwani Play – Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.

Use Auto Layout, reusable components, design tokens, accessibility best practices, responsive layouts, and realistic prototype interactions.

The experience should combine the professionalism of YouTube Studio, the organization of Notion, the collaboration of Frame.io, the publishing workflow of Vimeo OTT, and the simplicity of Canva—adapted for African creators.

Primary Goal

Create an end-to-end workspace where creators can:

Create projects
Organize productions
Upload videos
Manage seasons & episodes
Publish content
Monetize work
Track analytics
Collaborate with teams
Store production assets
Communicate with audiences
Manage creator earnings
Access AI production tools

The experience should support solo creators as well as professional production companies.

Expected Deliverable

Generate a complete creator operating system consisting of approximately 60–80 fully connected mobile screens, complete with reusable components, production workflows, cloud asset management, AI-assisted creation, monetization tools, analytics, collaboration features, publishing workflows, and interactive prototypes.

The result should feel like a professional SaaS product built specifically for Africa's creative industry while remaining fully integrated with the rest of the Pwani Play ecosystem.

🔷 Additional System Requirement

Every creator workflow must integrate seamlessly with:

Pwani Passport (verified identities and portfolios)
Pwani Wallet (earnings, payouts, and revenue)
Pwani Hub (casting, jobs, grants, and services)
Pwani Learn (creator education)
Pwani Connect (collaboration and networking)
Pwani AI (creative assistance)
Pwani Play (content publishing and streaming)

Design the experience as a single unified ecosystem where users move naturally between modules without feeling like they are switching applications. This interconnected approach is a defining characteristic of Pwani Play: Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.
