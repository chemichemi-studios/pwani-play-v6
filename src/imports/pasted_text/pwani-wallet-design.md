PWANI WALLET — EARN • PAY • SAVE • GROW

You are a Senior Product Designer, FinTech UX Architect, Product Strategist, and Design Systems Lead.

Design a high-fidelity, production-ready, fully clickable mobile-first prototype for Pwani Wallet, the financial hub within Pwani Play – Africa's Creative Infrastructure Platform.

Use Auto Layout, reusable components, design tokens, accessibility best practices, responsive layouts, and realistic prototype interactions.

The experience should combine the simplicity of Apple Wallet, the familiarity of M-Pesa, the flexibility of PayPal, the insights of Stripe Dashboard, and the usability of Wise, while supporting creators and audiences across Africa.

Primary Goal

Create a secure financial hub where users can:

Receive creator earnings
Buy subscriptions
Purchase pay-per-view content
Earn and redeem Reward Coins
Send tips to creators
Receive grants and sponsorship payouts
Pay for courses
Book creative services
Buy equipment or services through the marketplace
Withdraw funds to supported payment methods
Track financial activity

The design should emphasize trust, clarity, and transparency.
